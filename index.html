Dominik Liebl

Professor of Statistics · KU Eichstätt-Ingolstadt

Welcome … to my website. I am a Professor of Statistics at the Catholic University of Eichstätt-Ingolstadt.

My research follows an application-oriented approach, focusing on real-world data problems that are both statistically challenging and practically relevant. My current methodological and theoretical interests include functional data analysis, nonparametric statistics, longitudinal data analysis, as well as simultaneous and causal inference. In addition, I am strongly committed to providing computational implementations of my work and regularly publish R packages on CRAN and GitHub to accompany my research papers.

Teaching

A selection of past and current courses:

  • Computational Statistics / Data Science (B.Sc., M.Sc., PhD), University of Bonn. Statistical learning, regression analysis, classification, resampling methods, linear model selection and regularization, multiple testing, doubly robust machine learning, tree-based methods, functional data analysis.
  • Econometrics (M.Sc., PhD), University of Bonn. Probability theory, test theory, multiple linear regression, small- and large-sample inference, maximum likelihood inference, instrumental variable estimation, panel data analysis, time series analysis, causal inference.
  • Nonparametric Statistics (B.Sc.), University of Bonn. Rank statistics and tests, nonparametric density estimation, nonparametric hypothesis testing, nonparametric regression.
